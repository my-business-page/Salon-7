THE CUTETICLE EXPERIENCE

Cosy space.
Creative work.

A home-studio style appointment in Bishan designed around personal references, detailed nail work and a relaxed beauty session.

01 · Bring your idea

Public coverage describes Cuteticle SG as a design-forward home-based nail service with plenty of colours and patterns, including French tips and anime art.

02 · Talk through it

For a more involved set, send the reference ahead of time. This helps align on colours, length, complexity and timing before the appointment.

03 · Make it yours

The goal is not to reproduce a generic catalogue. Your screenshots, mood boards and favourite details become the starting point for the session.

04 · Add lashes

Nail services sit alongside lash and lash-lift services, so you can plan a more complete beauty reset around the same booking.
